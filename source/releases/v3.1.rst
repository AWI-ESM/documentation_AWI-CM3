AWI-CM3 v3.1
************

Changes to previous version
===========================
- Coupling Enthalphy of fusion of snow falling into open ocean `#80 <https://github.com/AWI-ESM/project_management/issues/80>`_.
- Coupling heat flux from divergence of SKT and T2M `#81 <https://github.com/AWI-ESM/project_management/issues/81>`_
- Upgrade from OpenIFS cy43r3v1 to cy43r3v2 `#84 <https://github.com/AWI-ESM/project_management/issues/84>`_ (Spectral Solar Insolation instead of Total Solar Iradiance)
- Weddell Sea Mixed Layer Depth & Polynya `#86 <https://github.com/AWI-ESM/project_management/issues/86>`_
- Putting melt pond albedo on better physical basis `#89 <https://github.com/AWI-ESM/project_management/issues/89>`_
- Gradient of surface sensible heat flux / wind speed vs (2m air temp - sea ice surf temp) `#90 <https://github.com/AWI-ESM/project_management/issues/90>`_
- Ocean current feedback coupling `#91 <https://github.com/AWI-ESM/project_management/issues/91>`_
- Coupling of mass and heat flux of snow falling on icesheets `#96 <https://github.com/AWI-ESM/project_management/issues/96>`_

Patch releases covered by this evaluation
=========================================

These install as their own targets and carry the fixes below, but they leave the evaluation unchanged, so the figures further down describe them too.

- ``v3.1.1``: fixed OpenIFS sources to allow for simulations with debug flags. `Tag 3.1.1 <https://gitlab.dkrz.de/ec-earth/oifs-43r3/-/commits/awicm-3.1.1>`_
- ``v3.1.2``: fixed a bug that caused a double orbital forcing call. `Tag 3.1.2 <https://gitlab.dkrz.de/ec-earth/oifs-43r3/-/commits/awicm-3.1.2>`_
- ``v3.1.3``: fixed a bug that made the ``L1PCTCO2`` switch for the one percent per year CO2 increase malfunction. `Tag 3.1.3 <https://gitlab.dkrz.de/ec-earth/oifs-43r3/-/commits/awicm-3.1.3>`_ This fix is in neither v3.2 nor v3.2.1.
